KnowMates Privacy Policy

KnowMates runs skill-exchange sessions between members. This page describes what we store and why.

Data we store

  • Account email and password (hashed)
  • Profile: display name, bio, avatar image, languages, timezone, birth date
  • Skills you teach or learn and your availability windows
  • Scheduled session times and optional backup meeting links you add
  • Text you send in session chat
  • Credit wallet ledger entries
  • Device push notification token (if you enable notifications)
  • Safety reports you file about other members

Calls

Audio and video during a call travel directly between the two phones. KnowMates stores session chat text only. We do not record calls.

Why we use it

To operate skill exchanges: matching, scheduling, messaging, credits, and safety.

Deletion

You can delete your account from Profile. That removes your account and chat lines you sent.

Sharing and ads

We do not sell your data. The app does not include advertising SDKs.

Camera and microphone

We request camera and microphone access only after you tap Join on a session call.
